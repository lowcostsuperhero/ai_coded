Airports likely to be busy, by hour of the day
==============================================

Your time is US Eastern (EST). Local time at each airport is in
brackets. The codes work directly with flightwall, for example:

   python flightwall.py HND

The airports are busy at those hours and well covered by the volunteer
receivers the free feed relies on (mainland China is left out for that
reason).

Patterns behind the list
------------------------
- ATL is busy from early morning to late evening, so it is the safest
  pick during US daytime.
- JFK peaks in the early evening, when the transatlantic departures
  leave.
- Europe's airports slow down from about 11 pm local, so they drop off
  after about 5 pm your time.
- Dubai (DXB) runs around the clock, and is a good backup if another
  airport seems quiet.

Caveats
-------
This is based on typical airline schedules, not live traffic, so check
with -o first. It holds until the clocks change: Europe moves in late
March, and the US, Australia and Japan-to-US gaps shift then too. After
that the European and Australian rows move by an hour.
